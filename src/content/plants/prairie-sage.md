Our Leksi Richard shared that Peji hota is a generic name for all the sages. There are six different kinds that belong to the aster family, including silver sage, women's sage (winyan tah pejuta), and men's sage (laskaka). Leksi Richard shared that women's sage can be used to doctor irregular menstruation. The aster family is big and also contains plants like sunflowers, echinacea, and purple asters too! The sage variety growing in this garden is prairie sage or ceremonial sage. He shared that during sun dances, after someone has been wounded, they will put sage in the wound to help it heal. Take a look below for more of its uses!

Note that some sages from California, like white sage, are actually from the mint family.
