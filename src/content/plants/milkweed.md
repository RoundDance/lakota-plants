Our Leksi Richard shared that in his opinion Wa'hcahca is one of the better food plants. You can eat the buds, flower heads, and seed pods, though you need to take out the seeds first. He read that when settlers were moving across the country a Lakota woman had a visitor scientist studying dinosaur bones. She invited him to supper and served a soup with wa'hcahca seed pods and buffalo meat.
